How it works

Drop a photo

The file is read by your browser and drawn to a canvas. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Faces are outlined

A face detector running in your browser finds faces and marks each one with a box and a count.

Pick an engine

Copy the image, open a search engine and paste. Results come from that engine, not from us.

Detection is not identification. Lens Atlas only shows where faces are in a picture. It never names anyone. Use reverse search to check where an image came from, and respect other people's privacy when you do.
